Cells are enclosed by a thick wall or membrane made of fats. This outer layer protects the inside of the cell from the dangerous external environment. Some things can pass through it, like water (H2O), but other things cannot. The cell also contains a central nucleus that houses DNA and RNA for genetic instructions. Mitochondria are also present inside the cell to generate power through respiration. I know proteins are involved in the cell somehow, but mostly the membrane just acts as a physical shield against viruses.
